PREAMBLE
1.0 We, the senior staff in the Petroleum, Natural Gas, Petrochemicals and Allied industries in Nigeria having firmly and solemnly resolved to from and belong to the Petroleum and Natural Gas Senior Staff Association of Nigeria (PENGASSAN) as one united and indivisible  body dedicated to the promotion of labour and industrial relations activities that would improve the welfare, (that is, the right, terms and conditions of our employment) and promote solidarity, peace , equity and justice amongst ourselves and other Nigerians, do hereby set for ourselves and adopt this constitution.
1.1 The acronym of the Association shall be PENGASSAN.
1.1.1 The symbol and logo of the Association shall be a drilling mast sandwiched by two metering  pumps enclosed in a circle with PENGASSAN written  beneath the mast
1.2 The registered office of the association shall situate in Lagos at U. M OKORO HOUSE 288, Ikorodu Road, Anthony, Lagos; or at such other place as the National Delegates’ Conference/Special National Delegates, Conference or the National Executive Council acting on behalf of the  National Delegates’ Conference may decide
1.3 The Association may establish and maintain Zonal offices such places as the National Delegates’ Conference/Special National Delegates’ Conference or the National Executive Council may decide
1.4 There shall be also branch offices situate at each of the individual petroleum, natural Gas, Petrochemicals and Allied Companies
1.5 The registrar of Trade Unions shall be notified in the prescribed form of any changes(s) in the registered office of the Association as may be decided by the National Delegates Conference or special National Delegates Conference of the National Executive Council acting on behalf of the National Delegates or Special National Delegates Conference.

2.0 SUPREMACY OF THE CONSTITUTION
2.1.   This document shall be unknown as the Constitution of the Petroleum and Natural  Gas Senior staff Association of Nigeria (PENGASSAN)
2.2. This Constitution is Supreme, and its provisions shall be binding on all members, authorities and persons in all the Offices of the Association.
2.3. If any provision of this Constitution is inconsistent with the provisions(s) of the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, or any enactment of the National Assembly, the provisions(s) of this Constitution to the extent of such inconsistency shall be null and void.
2.4. The Association shall be governed only in accordance with the provisions of this Constitution.
2.5 No part of this Constitution shall be amended, or any rule added except by simple majority votes of members at a National Delegates or special National Delegate’s conference.
2.6 Any proposal to amend any part of this Constitution must be forwarded to all members of NEC and all Branches of the Association at least twenty-one days to the date of the conference that will entertain such motion(s) for amendment.
2.7 No alteration or amendment to this Constitution by whatsoever process shall be deemed applicable until such amendment(s) or alteration(s) has been registered with the Registrar of trade Unions
2.8 This constitution shall apply as it across all the organs of the Association except where express provisions are made in this constitution on a particular subject matter
2.9 No Amendment to this constitution shall be applied retrospect
3.0 OBJECTIVES
The principal objects of the Association shall be as follows:
3.1 To accomplish the overall unionization of all Senior to Middle level Management Employees, whether temporary or permanent, in each Company/Organization in the Petroleum, Natural Gas, Petrochemicals and Allied Industries as members of the Petroleum and Natural Gas Senior Staff Association of Nigeria (PENGASSAN).
3.2 To enter into, regulate and maintain all forms of labour and industrial relations activities between the various Managements of the Petroleum, Natural Gas, Petrochemicals and Allied Companies all over Nigeria and their Senior to Middle level Management Employees on the one part and between two or more individual members or Branches of the Association on the other part.
3.3 To safeguard and improve the rights, terms and conditions of employment of its members through labour and industrial relations processes and activities.
3.4 To ensure that each Company's or Organization's Management provide the necessary occupational Health, environmental safety, social safety, and other welfare benefits which includes but not limited to Accident, disability, sickness, maternity, lost tools, authorization/payment for acquired tools where such tools are conditional to work performance, holiday, superannuation schemes, educational, amongst other benefits for members in themselves and for their families.

3.5 To enter into such collective investments that will improve the welfare of the Association and its members.
3.6 To promote the economic, social and educational welfare of the members in any lawful manner which the National Delegates' Conference /Special Delegates' Conference or the National Executive Council may from time to time deem expedient.

3.7 To inspire high standards of discipline, workmanship and professionalism at all times.
3.8 To encourage thrift and other savings schemes amongst members and adopt measures for promoting such savings schemes.
3.9 To render and to receive assistance and donations from other Trade Unions, Organizations and Institutions that support the growth and development of Trade Unionism.
3.10 To engage the Managements of Companies/Organizations and/or any level of Government on matters affecting the industry, labour relations, members and/or any other Staff or Group of Staff in the Petroleum, Natural Gas, Petrochemical, and allied Industries.
3.11 To advocate, canvass, promote and support policy and legislative processes and implementation with a view to establishing and protecting the rights and privileges of labour as well as for the introduction of benefits, welfare and social security measures.
3.12 To engage in activities that promote the wider interests of workers and to affiliate/amalgamate with other Trade Unions and Organizations that will assist the Association to achieve its objectives.
3.13 To ensure effective participation and representation in policy formulation, legislation and decision processes of Institutions, Petrochemical, Allied Boards, Agencies and Parastatals in the petroleum industry.
4.0 MEMBERSHIP
4.1 Each Company's/Organization's senior staff to middle level management within the Petroleum, Natural Gas, Petrochemical and allied Industries shall be eligible for membership of the Association regardless of race, gender and religion, provided he/she is 18 years of age and above.

4.1.1 A senior staff shall be an employee who possesses one or more of the following
i. specialized entry level qualifications like degree or Hiher diploma, Oridnary Diploma (depending on companies grading system)
ii. advanced expertied and experience
capability of working independently as specialist or  capacity to lead a technical or project team
4.1.2 A middle level manager shall be a person whose responsibility includes but not limited to
i. overseeing specific functions, team or department ii.
ii. Implementing strategies and policies, set by top/senior management
iii. Providing guidance and support to other employees
iv. Taking day to day  operational decisions and monitoring performance
v. Reporting to senior/top management/executives
4.2 The Association shall upon registration of member(s), ensure that employees and their management representatives recognizes the right to every registered member within the union’s jurisdiction to belong to the union without further assurance

4.3. In the event that a member is promoted, changes employment, wrongfully terminated , transferred or retired within the industry, the following shall apply.
(a)  Promotion out of PENGASSAN. Retain all membership right and privileges (except to vote and be voted for, and or representation of the Association)  for a maximum of two weeks at all organs of the association for transition.
(b) Changes Employment: Retain all membership rights and privileges in his new Branch. Where such a member is a National officer, he or she retain such position. If the person holds a position in  zone, it remains valid within that same zone. For change of employment outside zone or to a new Branch, he or she shall relinquish such position immediately.
Wrongful Termination: Retain all membership rights and privileges pending the final determination of the matter while still observing Rule 30.3.
Transfer: Retain all membership rights and privileges. If the person holds a position in the Association, it remains valid within the National/Zone/Branch. Where the transfer is across Branch or Zone, the person will relinquish his/her position after 90 days. Where the transfer is outside Nigeria, the position shall be declared vacant after 30 days and an election conducted to fill the position.
Study Leave: Retain all membership rights and privileges. If the person holds a position in the Association, the person will relinquish such position.
Retirement:
A retired member shall retain all membership rights and privileges for a period of seven (7) days. However, if he/she holds an elected position, such member must relinquish the position within seven (7) days period, even if such member has changed employment within the industry.
If the Member retires (before attaining the age of 60 or 35 years of service) and changes employment within the industry and is within the electable age, the member can contest election if he/she fulfils the eligibility requirements (refer to election guideline).
4.4 Acquisition, Merger, Split or Change of Name:
Employment changes arising from Acquisition, Merger, Split or Change of Name shall be treated as follows;
a) Acquisition: Where acquisition occurs; the acquired Branch and her officers shall cease to exist (except where PENGASSAN does not exist in the acquiring company) but the experience of the officers from the acquired Branch shall be considered in the fulfillment of electoral requirement for subsequent elections.

b) Merger: Where a merger results in a new Branch; fresh elections shall be conducted within three (3) months. During the three-month transition period, a Caretaker Committee with equal representation from both Branches shall be constituted to run the affairs of the new Branch. Where the legal status of the companies remain after the merger, the Branches will continue to exist.

c) Split: In the event of a split of a Branch to form two or more new Branches, bye elections shall be conducted within three months to fill the vacant positions in the new Branches so formed.
d) Change of Name: In the event of a change of name of a company, the subsisting officers of the Branch shall continue until the end of their tenure.
e) Acquisition of part of a branch: When part of a company (branch) is acquired, a caretaker committee will be set up in the part acquired and a bye election will be conducted to fill any vacant position in the remaining part except where over two-third of the officers are in the acquired part. A fresh election shall be conducted.

9.6 The NEC shall ratify the appointment of such staff of the Association as may be considered necessary.
9.7 The National Executive Council shall be the approving authority to investment and loans proposal(s) brought before it by the CWC.

9.10 The National Executive Council shall have the power to determine other specific financial contributions of members to the Association.

9.14 In both assessments as stated in 9.12 and 9.13, the Deputy President can only be removed by a 2/3 majority votes of the NEC-in-session.

10.0 CENTRAL WORKING COMMITTEE (CWC)
10.1 The Central Working Committee (hereinafter referred to as the “CWC”) shall comprise of the President, Deputy President, National Industrial Relations Officer, National Treasurer, National Financial Secretary, National Public Relations Officer, National Chairperson PENGASSAN Women Commission, Secretary PENGASSAN Women Commission, National Auditors, General Secretary, the Deputy General Secretaries, Zonal Chairmen and Zonal Secretaries of each of the Zones.
10.2 The CWC shall ensure an effective and efficient administration of the Association.

10.3 The CWC shall originate and recommend policy and procedure matters to the National Executive Council and National Delegates' Conference.

10.4 It shall implement the decisions of National Executive Council and National Delegates' Conference.
10.5 Members of the CWC shall not hold office at any other organ of the Association but shall be active ex-officio members of their respective Zones/Branches/Chapters/Units.
10.6 No Branch shall hold more than one of the following offices: President, Deputy President, National Industrial Relations Officer and National Treasurer. The same rule shall apply for the position of the Secretary in other organs/structures of the Association other than the CWC.
10.7 The CWC shall, for the effective performance of its oversight functions, have the powers to discipline member(s) and officer(s) of the Association at all levels, as shall become necessary and expedient, subject to the ratification of NEC.
10.8 The CWC shall on the recommendation of the Secretariat decide what employment positions should be created, maintained or terminated for the effective running of the Association including the management of the employees (except for General Secretary and Deputy General Secretaries which shall be subject to NEC ratification).
10.9 The CWC shall determine the terms and conditions of all employees of the Association.
10.10 The CWC may perform any other role(s) as may be deemed necessary for the smooth and effective running of the Association.

11.2 ELIGIBILITY
11.2.1 Only members who are within the ages of 21 years and 60 years shall be eligible to contest for positions in the Association.
11.2.2 Persons who have been disqualified by any clause of the Nigerian Constitution, extant Labour laws and/or this Constitution shall not be eligible to hold any office in the Association.
11.2.3 The incumbent Deputy President at NDC shall automatically transition to become President except as stated in rule 20.3.

11.2.4 To be eligible to contest the position of the Deputy President, a candidate must have completed at least a full tenure as a NEC and/or CWC member without prejudice to rule 20.4.
11.2.5 To be eligible for election into other positions of the Central Working Committee outside the positions mentioned in 11.2.3 above, a candidate must have served at least a full tenure as a member of Zonal Executive Committee or a Branch Executive Committee.
11.2.6 To be eligible for election into the other positions of the Zonal Executive Committee outside Zonal Chairman and Zonal Secretary, a candidate must have completed at least a full tenure as a member of either a Branch Executive Committee (BECOM) or Branch Executive Council (BEC).
11.2.7 To qualify for an elective position of Branch Chairman and Secretary, such a person must have served a full tenure as an elected officer of the Association in any Branch of the Association except where such a Branch/Chapter is a newly Inaugurated/approved Branch or Chapter.
11.2.7.1 For the purpose of elections as stated in Rule 11.2.6, a new Branch is a Branch that is less than three years old.
11.2.8 To qualify for other elective positions into Branch or Chapter or Unit Executive Committee, outside the Branch Chairman and Branch Secretary, such a person must have been a financial member of the Association for a period of 2 (two) years except where such a Branch or Chapter or Unit is a newly inaugurated/approved Branch or Chapter or Unit.
11.2.9 Only Candidates from Branches that have complied with Rule 6.7 shall be eligible to stand for election in a National or Special National or Zonal Delegates' Conference. Only candidates who are financially up to date shall be eligible to contest for Branch/Chapter/Units elections.
11.2.10 Only serving and former NEC member(s) who meet the eligibility criteria of Rule 11.2 are eligible to vote and be voted for, where there is any vacant position occasioned by the removal, dismissal, death, or resignation of a member of the Central Working Committee or other elected National Officer(s) in between National Delegates' Conferences. Except when the position of the president is vacant and the deputy president is sworn in immediately.
11.2.10.1 Such former NEC member(s) who meet the eligibility criteria of Rule 11.2 are eligible to vote and be voted for if they are nominated by their Branch to represent them.

11.8.4 If after the Electoral College round of voting and the candidates continue to tie, a die shall then be cast by the Returning Officer to determine the winner.

20.0 TENURE OF OFFICE
20.1 All elected officers of the Association shall hold office for one term of four years within the same organ. At the end of a term of four years, they may be re-elected into the same or any other office in that organ; except the President.
20.1.1 The President shall serve one single term of four years, except when the deputy president completes the previous president tenure as stated in 20.3.
20.1.2 The Deputy President shall serve a maximum of one term of four years when elected at NDC and shall not be eligible to re-contest into the position as he transition to become President except the deputy president comes into office through NEC filling as stated in 20.4 and the office of the president is not vacant at the next NDC as stated in 20.3.
20.1.3 A deputy president that emerge during NEC filling due to the transitioning of the previous deputy president to president; cannot become President in the next NDC because the office of the president is not vacant as stated in rule 20.3. 34.0 CODE OF PRACTICE
Every member of the Association whether holding any office or not and including every full-time employee shall observe the code of practice (as prescribed by the Federal Government of Nigeria) which is as set.
PREAMBLE
34.1 Although the primary objective of Trade Union is to further the economic interest of their members, they are basically an integral part of society and cannot realistically be considered outside it. They have become an important part of the economic fabric of Nigeria – recognized, respected and consulted by the Government and by Employers.
AIMS
34.2 The principal aims of this code of Practice therefore are:
(i) To encourage the positive contributions which the Trade Unions can make to the economic and social progress of Nigeria and her interest. To minimize and resolve the difficulties to which their her operations can give rise to, having regard to the laws, public policies and practice in force in the country.
(ii) To set standards and give practical guidance on the conduct of industrial relations and the development of policies to improve human relations in all types of employment.
(iii) To ensure and encourage at all levels the conduct of industrial relations by means of collective bargaining, consultation and effective communication between employers and their organizations and strong representative Trade Unions within a voluntary and legal machinery designed for the settlement of disputes and provision of adequate safeguards for the free association of workers on the one hand and employers on the other.
(iv) To develop a vigilant, informed and active membership, conscious of its right and interest and alive to its responsibilities in the operation of any Trade Union programme as an effective safeguard against abuse.
USE OF THE CODE FOR DISCIPLINARY ACTION
34.3 The failure on the part of any member and official of this Association to observe any of its provision(s) shall, in any proceedings for disciplinary action before a Committee or Tribunal under this Constitution be admissible in evidence and any provision of the code which appears to the Tribunal or Committee to be relevant to any question arising in the proceedings shall be taken into account by the Committee in determining the question at issue.
34.4 MEMBERSHIP RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES
Each member of a Trade Union should have the right to a full and free participation in the government of his Trade Union. This should include the right:
(i) To vote periodically, as provided in the Union's constitution and rules for Branch, District, Zone, State, Regional and National officers either directly or through delegates.
(ii) To honestly and democratically conduct election.
(iii) To stand for and hold office, subject only to fair and reasonable qualification uniformly imposed.
(iv) To express his/her views as to the method in which the Union's affairs should be conducted.
34.5 Each member should have the right to fair treatment in the application of Union rules and constitution. Trade Union disciplinary procedures should contain all the elements of fair play and the rules of natural justices, that is, notice, hearing and judgment on the basis of the evidence should be observed. A method of appeal to a higher body should be provided to ensure that judgments are not the result of prejudice or bias.
34.6 Each member has the responsibility, to fully exercise his/her rights of Trade Union membership and loyalty, to support his/her Union. The right of an individual member to criticize the policies of his/her Union and his/her Union officer does not include the right to undermine the Union as an institution, to destroy or weaken the Union as a collective bargaining agency, or to carry slander or libel of any officer of the Union.
34.7 Membership meetings should be held periodically, as provided for in the Union's constitution and rules with proper notice of time and place. It should be the responsibility of the Union to call such meetings and of the members to attend and participate actively.
PENALTY FOR BREACH
34.8 Sanctions for breaches of the code shall be fines, suspension from membership affiliations, office or employment and in the case of employee of the Union, termination of employment or dismissal. The amount of fine and appropriate penalty shall be left at the discretion of the Committee or Tribunal.

RESPONSIBILITIES OF ELECTED AND APPOINTED OFFICERS OF UNION
34.9.1 No person who has been convicted by a Court of competent jurisdiction of any crime involving moral turpitude, dishonesty and other criminal malpractices shall hold or retain elected or appointed office in the Association at any level.
34.9.2 No Trade Union official who already receives full pay from his Branch or Association shall receive fees or salaries of any kind from any other source save with the permission of the Branch or the Association.
34.9.3 No elected or paid Trade Union official shall have personal financial interest which conflicts with his full, effective and unalloyed performance of his duties as a worker's representative or a representative of the Association. In particular, no elected or paid Trade Union official or employee of either a Branch Union or the Association shall own a substantial interest in any business enterprise with which his Branch or Association bargains collectively or has interests and dealing with or in any other concern which is in competition with such enterprise or has considerable dealings with it.
34.9.4 All officials and staff of the Union shall -
34.9.4.1 Espouse the cause of workers and their Trade Union.
34.9.4.2 Uphold and defend the Constitution of the Union at all times.
34.9.4.3 Render honest, impartial and efficient services to the Union.
34.9.4.4 Serve as an effective link between the Union and its members.
34.9.4.5 Be trustworthy and responsible representative of workers to Management.
34.9.4.6 Process all legitimate grievances of workers in accordance with established procedures.
34.9.4.7 Obey all lawful instruction in the discharge of union function.
34.9.4.8 Eschew all forms of individualism which are inimical to the solidarity, stability and progress of Trade Union movement.
34.9.4.9 Put interest of the Union and its members above personal interest in the conduct of Union affairs.
34.9.4.10 Abide by majority decision and respect the right of minority.
34.9.4.11 Work for the growth of the Union in strength and freedom.
34.9.4.12 Further the cause of industrial democracy, peace, progress and social justice.
34.9.4.13 Render a true account of his/her stewardship to the members of the Union as and when the need arises.
34.10.0 FINANCIAL MATTERS
34.10.1 The fact that a Trade Union is a democratic organization implies that the members of the Union are entitled to assurance that the Union's funds, which are their funds, are not misappropriated. They are also entitled to be reasonably informed as to how the funds of the organization are being used or invested.
**34.10.2** Since a Union holds, funds in trust for the benefit of its membership and to further their interests, it should comply with standards generally applicable to fiduciaries or trustees with respect to the manner in which it keeps lists, records and accounts. Regular audits should be conducted and there should be appropriate distribution of summaries of such audits so that the membership and the public are adequately informed of the state of the organization finances.
34.11 CORRUPT PRACTICES
A basic ethical principle in the conduct of Trade Union affairs is that no
responsible Trade Union official should have a personal financial interest which conflicts with the full performance of his/her duties as a workers' representative or as representative of the Association and therefore of the Trade Union. All elected and appointed Trade Union officials as well as employees of the Association should eschew corrupt practices in all its forms, not only in financial and political matters, but also in those matters which involve loss of Union prestige and jeopardy of Union membership interests as well.
34.12 RACKETEERING
The decisive element in racketeering is personal enrichment at the expense of the Workers or Employers in the industry; for example; excessive demands, invasion of Management rights. Violence in trade disputes can be racketeering if the end result is the personal enrichment of an individual or group of individuals utilizing their strategic advantages in the Union for that purpose.
34.13 MISCONDUCT OF A BRANCH OF THE ASSOCIATION
If the activities of any Branch are in the opinion of the Association detrimental to the interest(s) of the Trade Union movement or contrary to the declared principles and policy of the Association, such activities shall warrant an investigation into the conduct of the Branch in accordance with the established procedure in the Constitution of the Association.
34.14.0 POLITICAL AFFAIRS
Members are encouraged to engage in political activities, however, participation should be done in such a way to avoid any form of conflict and the projection of the Association as being partisan.
34.14.1 Labour Party
A political party formed, mainly funded and whose leadership is drawn from Labour Union(s) including PENGASSAN.
34.14.2 Unless the rules of a Trade Union otherwise provide, in so far as that the funds of a Trade Union represent payment which the members are required to make under the rules, whether by way of subscriptions, dues or otherwise, funds shall not be applied (whether directly or indirectly) to the furtherance of any political objectives unless in the case of a Labour Party as defined in 33.14.1
34.14.3  Any member occupying elective/appointed position in any organ of the Association seeking to participate actively in politics to the extent of seeking an elective position shall resign his/her position in the Association unless in the case of a Labour Party as defined in 33.14.1
34.14.4 No elected/appointed officer of the Association should take up any leadership position in any political party and/or the Government at any level unless in the case of a Labour Party as defined in 34.14.1 without resigning his/her position in the Association.

34.15 UNION ELECTIONS
As bargaining representatives, Trade Union officials have power in conjunction with employers to fix workers' wages, hours and conditions of employment. It is therefore obligatory on all Trade Union members to ensure that officials who wield such power are responsive to the desires of the workers whom they represent. The responsiveness of Union officials depends upon the regularity of elections and the honest count of ballots. Every Trade Union official and member is therefore obliged not to obstruct the free and fair conduct of elections as and when they fall due in accordance with the Union's Constitution.

34.16 POACHING OR RAIDING OF UNION MEMBERS
Poaching or raiding of Union members by other Unions is contrary to Trade Union norms and a violation of Trade Union solidarity. It breeds antagonism among Unions, unrest, dissatisfaction, disunity among the workers involved, industrial strain and conflict. Above all, Poaching adds nothing to the strength and capabilities of the Trade Union movement as a whole.
34.17 As a general principle therefore, no worker who is or has recently been a member of any Trade Union should be accepted into membership of another Union without enquiry from his present or former Union. No member should be allowed to escape his financial obligations by leaving one Union, while in arrears and joining another. Neither should a Union accept members from any other Union, which is engaged in a trade dispute. Other circumstances should be considered on their merits.
35.0 GENERAL
The principal aim of Trade Unions is to promote their members' interests. They can do this only if the undertakings in which their members are employed prosper. They therefore have an interest in the success of those undertakings and essential contribution to make to it by co-operating in measures to promote increased productivity and efficiency. They also share with Management the responsibility for good industrial relations.
36.0 RATIFICATION
The amendments to this Constitution are hereby adopted and ratified as laws of the Petroleum and Natural Gas Senior Staff Association of Nigeria (PENGASSAN), on the 23rd day of Feb. 2026 Special Delegates Conference of the Association held in Abuja.

SCHEDULE 2: DISCIPLINARY CODE AND PROCEDURE
2.0 DISCIPLINARY CODE
2.1Any member or elected officer or organ may be disciplined by the Association for:
(i) Acting in a manner contrary to the Constitution of the Association.
(ii) Acting contrary to the interests of the Association or its members or any federation of Labor Unions to which the Association belongs.
(iii) Failure or refusal to carry out the legitimate decisions or orders of the Association without adequate reason(s) or gross negligence of duty.
(iv) Failing to be in good standing of the Association.
(v) Committing any other act(s) of misconduct.
2.2 DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE.
2.2.1 The Association has EGDC established at Branch, Zonal and National Organs in line with Rule 32.1.
2.2.2 Cases involving Members who are Zonal Officers in a Branch shall be referred to Zonal EGDC, while cases involving members who are CWC / National officers in a Branch shall be referred to the National EGDC.
2.2.3 In disciplining any member, elected officer or organ of the Association, the under listed procedure shall apply:
2.2.4 The member, elected officer or organ to be disciplined must be given at least 14 days written notice of the charges against him or her, the time and place for the hearing.
2.2.5 The relevant Grievance and Disciplinary Committee may proceed to hear and determine the charges if satisfied that:
  (i) the person charged is present
  (ii) although absent from the hearing, it is reasonably established that the person or organ charged received the notice and does not have any acceptable reason for not attending.
2.2.6 At the hearing of the charges, the person charged must have the opportunity to state his or her case personally and to call and question witnesses.
2.2.7 The EGDC shall review all evidences and establish the guilt or otherwise of the person charged and recommend appropriate disciplinary action(s) to the relevant organ that set up the EGDC.
2.2.8 The organ of the Association shall review the report of the EGDC implement the recommendation(s) accordingly except there is an appeal.
2.2.9 The person charged shall receive written notice of the decision.
2.3 APPEAL
2.3.1 Any person found guilty has the right to appeal within 7 days of the notice of the decision.
2.3.2 A person charged and found guilty in one level of the Association can appeal to the next higher level.
2.3.3 The Body receiving the appeal may confirm, vary or reverse the decision being appealed against.
2.3.4 Any member so suspended or disciplined in any other manner shall have the right to appeal to the National or Special National Delegates' Conference whose decision shall be final.
2.4.0 REMOVAL OF A CWC MEMBER FROM OFFICE.
2.4.1 Only the NEC-in Session can remove a national officer from office.
2.4.2 The NEC in Session shall consider the report of the EGDC and vote if necessary to remove from office a National officer by a two third majority.
2.4.3 The entire elected National officers can only be removed from office by National Delegates' Conference or a Special National Delegates' Conference convened for that purpose.
2.5.0 REMOVAL OF A ZECOM MEMBER FROM OFFICE
2.5.1 Only the ZEC-in Session can remove a Zonal officer(excluding Zonal Chairman and Zonal Secretary) from office
2.5.2 The ZEC- in Session shall consider the report of the EGDC and vote if necessary to remove from office a Zonal officer by a two third majority.
2.5.3 The entire elected Zonal officers can only be removed from office by Zonal Delegates' Conference or a Special Zonal Delegates' Conference convened for that purpose.
2.6.0 REMOVAL OF A BECOM MEMBER FROM OFFICE.**
2.6.1 The BEC/BECOM shall consider the report of the EGDC and vote if necessary to remove from office a Branch Officer (excluding the Branch Chairman and Branch Secretary) by a two third majority.
2.6.2 Only the General Congress -in Session can remove a Branch officer (excluding the Branch Chairman and Branch Secretary) from office.
2.6.2.1 The Branch Chairman and Secretary can only be removed by the ZECOM-in-Session.
2.6.3 The entire elected Branch officers can only be removed from office by Branch Delegates' Conference/a Special Branch Delegates' Conference/Conference as applicable, convened for that purpose.
SCHEDULE 3: INTERNAL DISPUTE RESOLUTION
3.0 The following shall be the Dispute Resolution Mechanism of the Association:
3.1 All internal disputes shall be subordinated through the following organs:
Unit - Chapter - Branch - Zone - CWC - NEC in that order.
3.2 A member who is aggrieved with the actions of a Unit/Chapter shall report such matter to the Branch Executive Committee who shall resolve such matter within two weeks.
3.3 A member who is aggrieved with the decisions or actions of a Branch Executive Committee shall report such matter to the Zonal Executive Committee. If such a member is from a Branch that is spread beyond one Zone, the matter shall be reported directly to the CWC who shall investigate and dispose of it within four weeks.
3.4 A member or Branch who is aggrieved with the decisions or actions of the CWC shall report the matter to NEC through the General Secretary.
3.5 A complaint by a member or Branch to the NEC shall be in writing through the General Secretary who shall be under obligation to forward such matter to the NEC. The NEC shall be at liberty to admit the complainant to state his/her case.
3.6 In the event that the aggrieved member is not satisfied with the resolution of the Branch Executive committee, such member can appeal to the Zonal Executive committee. If such a member is from a Branch spread beyond one zone, he or she can appeal directly to the CWC
3.7 A member/Branch who is not satisfied with the outcome of CWC intervention can appeal to NEC
3.8 A member//Branch who is not satisfied with the outcome of NEC intervention cana appeal to national Delegates’ Conference or special National Delegate’s Conference which decision shall be final
