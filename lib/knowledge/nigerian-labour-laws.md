## Who the Labour Act covers (important for senior staff)
- Labour Act, Cap L1 LFN 2004, s.91: "worker" excludes persons exercising administrative, executive, technical or professional functions.
  Many senior staff and middle managers may therefore fall outside parts of the Labour Act.
  Their rights then come mainly from their contract of employment, the company's conditions of service or handbook, any collective agreement negotiated by PENGASSAN, and the decisions of the National Industrial Court.

## Labour Act, Cap L1 LFN 2004
- s.7: Within 3 months of starting work, the employer must give the worker a written statement of the terms of employment (parties, nature of employment, wages, hours, holidays, notice, etc.).
- s.9(6): A contract must not make it a condition that a worker joins or does not join a trade union, or gives up union membership.
  An employer must not dismiss or otherwise prejudice a worker because of union membership or union activities outside working hours (or within working hours with the employer's consent).
- s.11: Minimum notice to end a contract (either side), unless the contract gives longer:
  - 1 day if employed for 3 months or less
  - 1 week if employed for more than 3 months but less than 2 years
  - 2 weeks if employed for 2 to 5 years
  - 1 month if employed for 5 years or more
  Notice of one week or more must be in writing. Either party may pay wages in lieu of notice.
- s.16: Up to 12 working days of paid sick leave a year, certified by a registered medical practitioner.
- s.18: After 12 months of continuous service, at least 6 working days of paid annual holiday.
- s.20: Redundancy. The employer must inform the trade union or workers' representative of the reasons and extent of the redundancy.
  Selection should follow "last in, first out" within the affected category, subject to skill, ability and reliability.
  The employer should use its best endeavours to negotiate redundancy payments.
- s.54: Maternity. At least 6 weeks' leave before and 6 weeks after confinement (12 weeks in total).
  If she has 6 months' continuous service, she is paid at least 50% of wages during this leave.
  She must not be dismissed or given notice during maternity absence. Nursing mothers get breaks to nurse their child.

## Trade Unions Act, Cap T14 LFN 2004 (as amended in 2005)
- Union membership is voluntary. No worker may be forced to join a union or to remain a member.
- Employers must deduct check-off dues from members' wages and remit them to the registered union.
- A union's funds may only be used for the purposes in its registered rules. Unions must file audited accounts with the Registrar of Trade Unions.

## Trade Disputes Act, Cap T8 LFN 2004
- s.4: Where the parties have agreed means of settling a dispute (for example a collective agreement or internal grievance procedure), those means must be used first.
- s.6: Where no agreed means exist, or they fail, the parties should meet within 7 days, with a mediator if necessary, to try to settle the dispute.
- If mediation fails, the dispute is reported to the Minister of Labour, who may appoint a conciliator.
  If conciliation fails, it goes to the Industrial Arbitration Panel (IAP). An objection to an IAP award can go to the National Industrial Court.
- Strikes and lockouts are restricted until the statutory dispute procedures have been exhausted. Members should never strike without union approval (see Constitution Rule 27).

## Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (Third Alteration) Act 2010, s.254C; National Industrial Court Act 2006
- The National Industrial Court of Nigeria (NICN) has exclusive jurisdiction over labour and employment matters.
  This includes unfair labour practice, discrimination and sexual harassment at work, and the application of international labour standards (ILO conventions and best practice).
- The NICN can find a dismissal unfair even where the contract allowed it, and can award compensation or order reinstatement.

## Employee's Compensation Act 2010
- Covers workers injured, disabled, made ill or killed in the course of employment, including occupational diseases and mental stress from work.
- The employer must report the injury or death to the Nigeria Social Insurance Trust Fund (NSITF). The worker or their dependants can claim compensation regardless of fault.

## Pension Reform Act 2014
- s.4(1): Minimum contributions are 10% of monthly emoluments by the employer and 8% by the employee, paid into the employee's Retirement Savings Account (RSA).
- An employer who fails to remit deducted pension contributions faces penalties. The employee can report this to PenCom.
- An employee who leaves employment before retirement age and is not re-employed within 4 months can withdraw up to 25% of their RSA balance.

## Practical routes for a member with a grievance
1. Raise it internally under the company's grievance procedure or conditions of service.
2. Report it to the Branch Executive Committee (Branch IRO). Within PENGASSAN, escalate as in Schedule 3: Unit → Chapter → Branch → Zone → CWC → NEC.
3. Industrial disputes go through the Trade Disputes Act route: mediation, then the Ministry of Labour, then the IAP, then the NICN.
4. Legal assistance from the Association is available on the Branch Executive Committee's recommendation and with CWC approval (Constitution Rule 30.2).
